PM5 data explorer

Connect, row, and see what the erg sends. Back to the game

Connect your PM5. To test heart rate straight from a chest strap, watch, or other heart rate monitor, connect that too.

Bluetooth needs Chrome or Edge on a laptop, or Chrome on Android. Safari and iPhone can't connect.

Close other apps that use the PM5 first, like ErgData. A chest strap works as is. A watch must be broadcasting heart rate over Bluetooth. Straps often allow only one or two connections, so to test the PM5's own heart rate first, leave the heart rate monitor button alone.
