Connect your PM5. To test heart rate straight from a chest strap, watch, or other heart rate monitor, connect that too.

Bluetooth needs Chrome or Edge on a laptop, or Chrome on Android. Safari and iPhone can't connect.

Close other apps that use the PM5 first, like ErgData. A chest strap works as is. A watch must be broadcasting heart rate over Bluetooth. Straps often allow only one or two connections, so to test the PM5's own heart rate first, leave the heart rate monitor button alone.

Stroke Data

Power curve

Waiting for the first stroke. The PM5 sends the shape of each pull as a stream of points, and this graph draws the latest stroke from them.

Units: pounds of force (lbf), the same as the "lbf" label on your PM5's graph. Your PM5 can also show newtons ("N"): 1 lbf is about 4.45 N. Left to right is one drive: legs first, then the body swing, then the arms. The PM5 only sends the curve after each drive, so it appears just after you finish the stroke and grows as it arrives. The faint line is your last full stroke.
